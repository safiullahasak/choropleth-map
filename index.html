ABOUT THIS MAP

Sources & method

Population counts come from Table 1 of the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics’ Population and Housing Census 2023 for Balochistan. The province total shown is 14,894,402.

The district outlines come from the public Pakistan administrative GeoJSON boundary set. Three census districts created after those outlines were produced are grouped with their parent outline: Chaman with Killa Abdullah, Duki with Loralai, and Surab with Kalat. Their population and land area are summed for those map areas.

Density is calculated as population divided by the summed land area. This map is a local demonstration and is not an official Government of Balochistan product.
